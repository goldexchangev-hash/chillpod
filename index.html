Does It Actually Work, or Is It Just a Pouch?+
It actively removes heat — a pouch only insulates. The frozen core sits against your phone so heat flows out into the core. In our sauna test: about 8 minutes bare vs 30 minutes in iChillPod.
What Phones Fit?+
The phone-shaped slot fits modern iPhone and Android handsets. Fits all phones smaller than the iPhone 17 Pro Max (163.4 × 78.0 × 8.75 mm). That model is the size ceiling — anything larger may not fit.
How Long Does One Freeze Last?+
Long enough for a beach trip, a round of golf, or a full game. Up to seven hours in a gym bag. Refreezes overnight and reuses all summer.
Will It Get My Phone Wet?+
Condensation can form on the cooler as the frozen core warms. Wipe it off before you pocket the phone.
